About Me

I am currently a junior at Arizona State University pursuing a Computer Science degree with a Mathematics minor. I am currently seeking Summer 2027 internships.

My passion for software engineering is fueled by the joy of problem-solving and the thrill of creating solutions. I thrive on the challenge of breaking down complex problems into manageable pieces and crafting efficient solutions. I am particularly drawn to backend development, where I can design robust systems that power user experiences. I believe that software engineering is not just about writing code, but about building systems that can grow and adapt over time and I am always eager to learn new technologies and methodologies to enhance my skills.

Outside of academics, I serve on two committees in Alpha Chi Omega and I'm also active in the Society of Women Engineers and Women in Computer Science, where I'm constantly learning from and connecting with other women in tech.
